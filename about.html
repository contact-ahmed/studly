About Studly.

Your simple companion for smarter student life and better organization.

Why Studly?

Student life can become stressful when you have multiple assignments, projects, quizzes and deadlines to manage. Studly was created to make academic task management easier and more organized. Our goal is to help students stay focused, manage their time and complete their work without forgetting important deadlines.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with a simple and accessible platform where they can organize their academic activities, track their progress and develop better productivity habits.

What Can You Do With Studly?

Manage Tasks

Add and organize assignments, projects and academic activities.

Track Progress

Monitor completed and pending tasks through your dashboard.

Stay Organized

Keep track of deadlines and prioritize your academic work.
